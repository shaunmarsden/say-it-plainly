## Meeting and call notes

The reader may be you, in a month, or someone who was not there. They will act on what you wrote, so the notes have to say how much each line can be trusted.

- **Say who said it.** "Ines says feedback is positive" and "feedback is positive" are different notes.
- **Keep three things apart:** what the meeting settled, what still needs checking, and what the notes disagree about. Put each line in one place, and do not file an impression under "settled". Use a heading only if a line belongs under it. If the notes record nothing as settled, leave that heading out.
- **Keep an impression as an impression.** "Reckons", "seems" and "looks like" stay in the note, with the name of the person who thought it.
- **Record a number with its source and its date.** "61,000 pounds spent as at the 5 September finance report" is a note. "Around 70,000" is a guess until a report says so.
- **Separate a decision from a discussion.** Write "decided" only if the notes say someone made the call, and say who if they do.
- **Give every action an owner and a date,** or write "the notes do not name an owner" and "the notes do not give a date". Do not leave the gap quiet.
- **A gap in the notes is not a fact about the meeting.** The notes may record no decision, no owner or no source when the meeting had one. Write what the notes do not say. Do not write that nothing was decided, that nobody took something on, or that no one knew.
- **When two lines disagree, show both.** Do not settle the disagreement in the notes. Note who could settle it.
- **Use the writer's own word for each thing.** If the notes say "the review", "the check-in" or "the call", do not write "review meeting" or "next meeting". Do not turn a thing into an event, a request into an agreement, or an estimate into a fact.
- **Do not add what nobody said.** A note that fills a gap with a plausible detail is worse than a note with a gap. Do not add a cause ("because", "depends on"), an action, a follow-up or an open question that the notes do not contain. Put your own questions in "What I need from you".

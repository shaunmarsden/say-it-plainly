## Emails and messages

The reader is busy and may read this on a phone between other things. They should know what you want from them within two lines.

- **One ask.** If you need two things, send two messages, or say which one matters most. Put the ask in the first two lines.
- **The subject line says what you need.** "Can you confirm the 14th by Friday?" beats "Quick question".
- **Answer first when you are replying.** Give the answer, then the reason.
- **Say when, not "soon".** Name the day, the time or the date.
- **Short paragraphs, one idea each.** Lists only for a real list of items.
- **Do not apologise for writing,** and do not thank someone in advance for something they have not done.
- **Cut the opener and the closer** if they only fill space: "Hope you're well", "Just checking in", "Please don't hesitate to".
- **Keep it short.** Most emails work in under 150 words. If yours is longer, check that every paragraph earns its place.

Thrilled to share some learnings from my AI journey!

For the last two months I've been using an AI tool to summarise my customer calls. 140 calls so far.

The good: by my rough timing on 20 of those calls, it saves me about six minutes a call. I haven't timed the rest.

The not so good: it got the next steps wrong in 9 of the 140 summaries, usually when two people were talking at once. And once it put a complaint against the wrong customer. A colleague caught that one before it went into our CRM.

So I still read every summary before I save it, and I don't plan to stop.

Two of the four people in my team stopped using it after a week. They found checking the summary slower than writing their own notes.

I've only tried one tool, so I honestly don't know whether another one would do better.

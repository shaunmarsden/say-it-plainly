For two months I've used an AI tool to summarise my customer calls. So far that's 140 calls.

What worked: it saves me about six minutes a call. That's from timing 20 of the calls. I haven't timed the other 120.

What didn't:
- It got the next steps wrong in 9 of the 140 summaries, usually when two people spoke at once.
- Once it put a complaint against the wrong customer. A colleague caught it before it reached our CRM.

Because of that, I read every summary before I save it, and I don't plan to stop.

Two of the four people on my team stopped using it after a week. They found checking the summary slower than writing their own notes.

I've only tried one tool, so I don't know whether another would do better.

Has anyone else tried this? What did you find?

For the last two months I've been using an AI tool to summarise my customer calls. 140 calls so far.

By my rough timing on 20 of those calls, it saves me about six minutes a call. I haven't timed the rest.

It got the next steps wrong in 9 of the 140 summaries, usually when two people were talking at once. Once it put a complaint against the wrong customer. A colleague caught that one before it went into our CRM.

So I still read every summary before I save it, and I don't plan to stop.

Two of the four people in my team stopped using it after a week. They found checking the summary slower than writing their own notes.

I've only tried one tool, so I don't know whether another one would do better.

**What I changed**

- Cut the opener "Thrilled to share some learnings from my AI journey!" The post now starts with the fact.
- Cut "The good:" and "The not so good:". The content says which is which.
- Cut the closing "What's your experience been? Drop a comment below!" It asks for comments and adds nothing.
- Cut the four hashtags. None of them helps a reader find or understand the post.
- Cut "honestly" from the last line. The sentence works without it.
- Changed "And once it put" to "Once it put", and joined the sentence about the colleague to the one before it by keeping them adjacent as one thought.

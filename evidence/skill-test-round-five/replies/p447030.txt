For the last two months I've used an AI tool to summarise my customer calls. 140 so far. I still read every summary before I save it, and I don't plan to stop.

It saves me about six minutes a call. That is my rough timing on 20 of the calls. I haven't timed the rest.

It got the next steps wrong in 9 of the 140 summaries, usually when two people were talking at once. Once it put a complaint against the wrong customer. A colleague caught that before it went into our CRM.

Two of the four people in my team stopped using it after a week. They found checking the summary slower than writing their own notes.

I've only tried one tool, so I don't know whether another one would do better.

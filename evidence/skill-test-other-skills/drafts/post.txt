I spent three weeks testing whether AI can draft our monthly client reports.

Honest answer: sometimes. It got the structure right every time. It got a figure wrong in 2 of the 11 reports, both times a number that belonged to a different client's file. I don't know why. Maybe the prompt, maybe the tool.

So now a human checks every number before anything goes out.

Subject: Your order 5521 is delayed

Your parcel has been delayed at our depot. This was our fault: we had a staffing problem.

We think it will reach you by the 12th, but the carrier has not confirmed that date.

If it has not arrived by the 15th, we will refund the £6.50 delivery charge. We will not refund the cost of the item.

What I changed:
- The subject now says what the email is about.
- I cut the opening line ("I just wanted to reach out"), so the delay comes first.
- I split it into short paragraphs: the delay, the date, the refund, then the question. Your question is now on its own, so it is hard to miss.
- I dropped "Unfortunately" and the passive "was down to". Saying "this was our fault" is plainer and clearer about who is responsible.
- I kept every fact as you had it: the 12th is an estimate only, the refund applies only to the delivery charge, and the deadline for the address is Friday.

One thing to check: "Friday" is ambiguous if the email is read late in the week. Adding the date, for example "by Friday 9th", would make it clearer. I did not add one because I don't know the date.
